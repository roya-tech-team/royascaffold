# Feature questions

> **What:** questions asked while planning a feature, and the person's answers
> **Read when:** planning or approving a feature, or wondering why a requirement says what it says

Added with `royascaff new record question "<question>" --feature CAP-…`. A feature cannot be approved while one of its questions has no answer.

## Record format (example)

```md
### QST-{{CODE}}-004 · Should results show numbers or percentages?

- **Feature:** CAP-{{CODE}}-001
- **Answer:** both: the number first, the percentage in brackets
- **Source:** chat 2026-09-30
```
