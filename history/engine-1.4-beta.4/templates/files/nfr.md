# Quality requirements

> **What:** qualities the product must keep: speed, security, accessibility, reliability
> **Read when:** designing or checking anything that could make the product slower, less safe or harder to use

## Record format (example)

```md
### NFR-{{CODE}}-001 · Results load fast

- **Priority:** must
- **Owner:** {{OWNER}}
- **Feature:** CAP-{{CODE}}-001

**Measure:** the Results page shows data within 2 seconds on a 4G connection.
```
