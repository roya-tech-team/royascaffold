# Pattern Decision Template

For each material `PAT-*` include standard metadata plus Decision status, Materiality,
Problem/forces, selected adapter provenance, chosen pattern, why it fits, constraints,
failure modes, rejected alternatives, affected criteria/components/contracts/tasks,
validation method, and residual risk.

The record makes a project choice; adapter defaults are not architecture facts. A
pattern may satisfy or constrain an outcome but cannot replace a `CRIT-*`.
