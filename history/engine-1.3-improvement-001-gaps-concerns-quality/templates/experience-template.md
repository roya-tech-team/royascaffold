# Experience and Interface Template

Include experience principles and priority; actor journeys; first-view focus; information
and navigation hierarchy; composition/density/scale; screens/interactions/forms;
state/permission behavior; loading/empty/error/success/unauthorized states; typography,
color, motion, feedback, accessibility, responsive/input/localization rules; content
tone/credibility; RDR retain/adapt/reject links; unacceptable outcomes; component/
action/contract/pattern links; and scenario/visual evidence requirements.

Use explicit decisions and criteria rather than adjectives such as “premium” without
observable meaning. Main must retain enough durable experience rules to rebuild the
approved result without the original reference conversation.
