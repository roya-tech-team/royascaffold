# Solution Quality Review Template

Header: `REV-*` ID, change, reviewer/authority, timestamp, and exact reviewed input
IDs/revisions/fingerprints.

Review intent/reference fidelity, cross-artifact coherence, feasibility, quality-bar
authority, material decision/alternative completeness, foundation priority, content/
data credibility, adapter coverage/conflicts, likely first-pass risks, and assumptions.

Record findings with severity, owner, owning source, correction, and closure evidence.
Disposition: `pass`, `pass-with-conditions`, or `revise`. Fix source owners and re-review;
do not hide design changes in this report.
