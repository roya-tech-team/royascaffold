# Workflow Template

For `WF-*` include standard metadata plus purpose, actors, trigger, preconditions, states, transitions, invariants, happy path, alternative/failure/timeout/retry/compensation behavior, events/side effects, owning requirements/components/actions/tests, and Mermaid state/activity/sequence diagram.

Create when multiple actors/apps, >3 states, approvals, async/retry/compensation, sensitive transitions, or important business value applies.
