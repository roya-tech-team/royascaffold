# Verification Template

Sections: change/source/canonical/QDC revisions and fingerprints; changed-file inventory
and task-scope result; deterministic command results; `CRIT-*`/requirement/invariant/NFR
evidence matrix; expected/actual; replay method; environment; artifact; executor and
adjudicator authority; freshness; contract/architecture/security/data/content/
experience/operations checks; semantic findings; limitations; not-applicable reason;
repair links; Overall PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE/MANUAL-REQUIRED/STALE.

PASS without fresh, source-bound evidence and permitted authority is invalid. Missing
runner is `manual-required`; a design defect returns to its owning pre-code artifact.
