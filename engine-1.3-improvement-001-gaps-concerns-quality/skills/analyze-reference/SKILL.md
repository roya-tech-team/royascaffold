# Analyze Reference

Use only when a source materially influences the desired outcome.

1. Record stable source identity/fingerprint, intended use, authority, and rights limits.
2. Separate observed `FND-*` facts from interpretation; record confidence and ambiguity.
3. Analyze only applicable dimensions. For interfaces this may include composition,
   hierarchy, density, scale, color, typography, motion, interaction, responsiveness,
   accessibility, tone, and content. For non-visual work use behavior, contract, data,
   compatibility, or operational dimensions.
4. Create `RDR-*` decisions for material aspects: `retain`, `adapt`, `reject`, or
   `unresolved`. Explain why and the unacceptable opposite.
5. Link decisions to `DEC-*`, observable `CRIT-*`, design/pattern inputs, and eventual
   tasks. Do not replace outcomes with vague adjectives or mechanisms.
6. Escalate material ambiguity to its authority. Do not infer approval to copy protected
   assets or implementation details.

Output belongs to the active Change until durable decisions reconcile into Main.
