# Reconcile Drift Workflow

1. Detect and report difference without choosing a semantic winner.
2. Classify: undocumented code, stale knowledge, incomplete change, defect, generated drift, or intentional exception.
3. Route ambiguous intent to the owning developer/domain/architecture/security role.
4. Use a knowledge-only reconciliation or a remediation Change.
5. Preview canonical changes including durable QDC criteria, reference/pattern
   decisions, assumption closure/supersession, and affected evidence.
6. Apply atomically; regenerate and validate the Main rebuild test.
7. Archive QDC/RDR/SQR/IRR/context provenance without promoting generated views or
   review dispositions as product truth.

Timestamps alone never decide requirement meaning.
